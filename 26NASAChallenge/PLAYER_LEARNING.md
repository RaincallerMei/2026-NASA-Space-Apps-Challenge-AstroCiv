# Player learning checks

These checks document outcomes already requested in the research brief and board. They do not change gameplay or dialogue. No player results have been collected.

| Outcome | Observable check | Existing feature |
|---|---|---|
| Predict battery depletion | Given 4 kWh and a 1 kW deficit, predicts four game hours | Board battery example and power tests |
| Identify critical versus optional loads | Keeps air/life support on and names a load that can wait | Existing priorities and optional toggles |
| Distinguish radiation from shadow | Says shelter/recall handles particles; shadow reduces solar power | Separate storm and illumination systems |
| Explain the greenhouse loop | Names power and water as inputs, food as output, and says recycling saves water | Existing greenhouse and CSA cards |
| Connect the story to a real first step | Names studying science/engineering or building teamwork skills; understands astronaut selection is not guaranteed | Existing career disclaimer and the CSA requirements source |

Crew-chat check: a child can choose a portrait and a reply button without typing, finish without a dead end, and describe how one fictional crew member felt. Later ask whether they noticed the geologist remembering a storm choice. Record the player's actual words.

Use the five-minute script and blank results table in [TEST_PLAN.md](TEST_PLAN.md). Observe before helping, record help, and keep short usability sessions distinct from the full 12–16 minute playthrough.

Accessibility requirements already agreed: mouse-first controls, large buttons, simple sentences, text plus icons/shapes plus colour, Pause, scalable UI, no live AI chat and no required physics background. Visual compliance still needs review in the completed screens.

Career source checked November 14, 2026: [CSA astronaut requirements](https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/astronauts/how-to-become-an-astronaut/requirements-and-conditions.asp). Education and relevant experience are requirements, not a promise of selection.
