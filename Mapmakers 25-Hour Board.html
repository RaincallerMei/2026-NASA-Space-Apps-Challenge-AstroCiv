Big pictureThis is not a resource game with a story on top. It is a story about a dream of exploring space. Managing power, air, water and food is how the player lives that dream.

Every great civilization starts with a dream.

A child who loved building civilizations on a computer gets to build humanity's first real lunar settlement.

Three ideas

Wonder

What does it feel like to look at the Moon and imagine living there?

Felt in: bedroom, the drawing, the sunrise.

Responsibility

What does it take to keep people alive in an unfamiliar world?

Felt in: power, air, water, food, crew chat, the storm.

Possibility

Could a kid from Mississauga become part of humanity's space story?

Felt in: Hadfield and Seager moment, photo cards, final message.

Honesty rules for the story

  • Seager and Hadfield: names as inspiration only. No faces, no quotes, no hint they back the game. Check every fact on their own pages.
  • Seager was TESS Deputy Science Director. Not the director.
  • Say once: the astronaut path is a dream, not a promise.
  • Science event, teacher and crew are made up. Say so.
  • Write "Earth hangs low above the horizon". Do not write "Earthrise". From most Moon spots Earth barely moves.
  • Storm and sunrise are separate. A solar warning never turns panels off.
  • Game numbers (kW, kWh, costs) are made-up balance numbers. Label them.
  • Story text under about 650 words. Lines up to 14 words.

Make Midjourney look like real pixel art

  • Ask for: "16-bit pixel art, top-down, limited palette, flat colours".
  • Shrink to 480 × 270 with nearest-neighbour. Scenes then scale ×4 to 1080p.
  • Crew portraits: ask for a face, then shrink to 32 × 32 and clean by hand.
  • Cut colours to the same 16 to 32 as the tile pack.
  • Write "AI-made art" in ASSET_CREDITS.md and on the project page. Never credit it to NASA.
