## Rights and public repository use

- [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) and [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) are different: CC BY requires attribution and an indication of changes. Neither requires paying for the uses its terms permit.
- Incompetech's [licensing page](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/licenses/) explicitly offers a free attribution route. A paid no-attribution license is optional. Generate the exact credits for each downloaded title; preserve any license version attached to that copy.
- Keep third-party music/SFX under their own terms. A public GitHub repository does not turn everything into public-domain material. Add per-file credits/license notices before committing any downloaded audio.
- Suggested credit pattern: `"TITLE" by CREATOR — SOURCE URL — LICENSE + LICENSE URL. Changes: trimmed, looped and gain adjusted.` For CC0, preserve the same provenance even though attribution is not required.
- Store the original file, source URL, retrieval date, license evidence, edits and final filename in the asset record. Verify that each selected file matches its source entry.

## Proposed integration and audition checklist

1. Audition menu, cutscene and gameplay candidates at similar loudness. Pick one per context; do not ship the whole shortlist.
2. Test 30 seconds of dialogue over each cutscene candidate and at least three repetitions of a gameplay loop. Use crossfades/loop edits only where licensed; do not assume an MP3 is seamless.
3. Keep menu music calm, cutscene music a little fuller, gameplay music lightly pulsing. Add a separate sparse tick only near evaluation, tied to the game clock and suspended when paused. Do not speed up the whole mix at 2x/4x.
4. Provide independent music/SFX volume and mute. Throttle repeated warnings; use visual equivalents for all important sounds.
5. Prefer lossless working masters, short mono WAV effects, and compressed stereo music suitable for Unity WebGL. Check download size, clipping, seams and browser audio unlock after the player's first click.
6. Update `26NASAChallenge/ASSET_CREDITS.md` and the AI-use log **when assets are actually selected/generated/imported**. This research pass does not establish that any audio is used in the game.
